Research Interests

Science of artificial intelligence, statistical physics of neural systems, theoretical neuroscience and dynamical systems, memory and cognition in humans and machines.

Current Projects

  • Science of Artificial Intelligence: Building theory for different facets of language modeling. This includes a renormalization group analysis of tokenization compression algorithms, and exploring symmetries of tokenized large language models.
  • Statistical Mechanics of Human Memory: Understand human memory on narrative recall tasks, with a combination of experiment and theory. Involves the use of AI for quantitative analysis of language data. Also includes developing statistical mechanical models to explain experimental data, and making testable predictions for future experiments.
